# NexusEdu

Guided AI Learning Platform for Vietnamese High School Students

---

## Overview

NexusEdu is a structured learning platform for Vietnamese high school students who want to explore Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Computer Science (CS).

Students today can access information easily but lack structure, guidance, and real understanding. NexusEdu provides a clear pathway to learn, apply, and reflect.

---

## Problem

Vietnamese students interested in IT face a mismatch:

- School IT curriculum is outdated and lacks real-world relevance
- Online courses are difficult, expensive, and mostly in English
- AI tools provide answers but weaken independent thinking
- Self-study leads to:
  - Procrastination
  - Information overload
  - Lack of feedback
  - Shallow understanding

Data:
- 69.7% of students want guidance on using AI effectively
- Only ~30% of IT graduates in Vietnam are job-ready

---

## Solution

NexusEdu is a guided AI-powered learning system.

It is not a chatbot. It is a structured environment that forces active thinking.

Learning flow:
1. Attempt
2. Hint
3. Revision
4. Reflection

Students must try before receiving help.

---

## Key Features

### For Students

**Foundation Courses**
- Built for high school level
- Focus on core AI, ML, CS concepts
- Save time and build strong fundamentals

**Dual-Language Bridge**
- Start in Vietnamese
- Gradually transition to English
- Learn technical vocabulary naturally

**Progression Tree**
- Personalized learning path
- Shows connections between topics
- Tracks progress and ranking

**Reflection Jar**
- Weekly reflection system
- Identify mistakes and misconceptions
- AI generates personalized follow-up tasks

**Discussion Hub**
- Ask questions and share answers
- Find teammates and mentors
- Engage in technical discussions

**Weekly Cases**
- Real-world problems from educators
- AI provides hints and background
- Students collaborate on solutions

---

### For Educators

**Credit Accumulation**
- Track volunteer hours
- Earn milestones (Bronze, Silver, etc.)
- Use as portfolio evidence

**Unanswered First Filter**
- Highlights unanswered or weak questions
- Focus effort where it matters most

**Co-Teaching Threads**
- Collaborate with other educators
- Improve answer quality

**Case Study Creator**
- Generate structured case studies
- Set difficulty and hints
- Reduce preparation time

---

## Target Users

- Vietnamese high school students (16–18)
- Interested in AI, ML, CS
- Lack structured guidance
- Especially in public or low-resource schools

---

## Technology Approach

- Uses existing LLMs for:
  - Hint generation
  - Bilingual explanation
  - Reflection prompts

- Core content is:
  - Curated
  - Teacher-reviewed
  - Controlled to reduce hallucination

---

## Pilot Plan

**Timeline:** May 2026 – May 2027

**Scale:**
- 3 high schools
- 150–180 students
- 20 volunteer educators

**Phases:**
1. Validation and research
2. Prototype development
3. Teacher onboarding
4. Pilot and evaluation

---

## Risk Management

**Data and ownership**
- Clear policies
- Consent-based usage
- Secure storage

**AI hallucination and content quality**
- Admin moderation
- Educator verification
- Controlled AI prompts

---

## Vision

- Prepare students early for tech careers
- Reduce gap between school and industry
- Build real problem-solving skills
- Create a strong learning community
